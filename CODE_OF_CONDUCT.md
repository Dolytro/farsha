# Code of conduct

Farsha follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## In short

- Be kind and respectful. Welcome people of every background and experience level.
- Give and accept feedback on the work, not the person.
- No harassment, insults, discrimination, or personal attacks. No sexualised language or attention.
- Do not publish other people's private information.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, issues, commits and pull requests that break these rules, and may
temporarily or permanently ban anyone whose behaviour they judge inappropriate.

To report a problem, use GitHub's private **Report a vulnerability** form on the Security tab and mark the
report as a conduct issue, or contact the repository owner through their GitHub profile.
All reports are handled privately and fairly.
