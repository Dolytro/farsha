Farsha Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026

Farsha is a free, open source design tool at canvylo.vercel.app. This page explains what happens to your data when you use it.

What we collect

Farsha has no server of its own and no database. We do not collect, store or sell your personal data, and we do not use analytics or advertising trackers.

Google sign-in and Google Drive

You sign in with your Google account. Farsha asks for one permission, drive.file, which lets the app see and edit only the files it creates (or that you open with it) in your own Google Drive. It cannot see your other Drive files.

Your designs are saved as .farsha files in your Google Drive. Your name, email address and profile photo are read from Google to show who is signed in. They stay in your browser and are not sent anywhere else.

The sign-in token is kept in your browser session and expires after about one hour. Signing out removes it.

Sharing

If you turn on "Anyone with the link can view", Farsha adds a view-only link permission to that file in your Google Drive. Anyone with the link can view the design. You can turn this off at any time, which removes the permission.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Farsha's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Your choices

You can delete your .farsha files from Google Drive at any time, and you can revoke Farsha's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
